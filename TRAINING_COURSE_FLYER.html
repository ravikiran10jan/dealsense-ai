What Makes This Different
  • You build a real app, not toy examples. The case study is DealSense AI — a production-grade sales assistant with a FastAPI backend, React frontend, and live AI features.
  • Honest about what AI can and can't do. We talk about hallucinations, limitations, and when NOT to use AI. No magic, no hand-waving.
  • Beginner-friendly, not beginner-only. If you know basic Python, you're ready. Experienced developers will pick up architecture patterns and production best practices.
  • Curiosity over fear. This course exists because AI is genuinely interesting and useful — not because you should be scared of it.
  • You leave with working code. Every concept comes with real code from the DealSense AI repo that you can study, modify, and reuse in your own projects.
